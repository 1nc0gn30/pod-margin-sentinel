Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

1. Zero Financial Data Retention

Your store's private cost structure, profit margins, supplier wholesale discounts, and retail pricing strategies are calculated in-memory directly in your web browser. We never store, log, or sell your commercial cost data.

2. Payments & Licensing

Transactions are processed via Stripe or DePay Solana smart contracts. Your permanent license token is securely stored in your browser's localStorage for instant offline access.

3. Third-Party Trackers

We do not embed third-party advertising trackers or invasive analytics pixels. Operational telemetry is restricted to local server uptime and anonymous aggregate calculation metrics.
