License Key Reactivation

If you switch computers or clear your browser cache, click "Have a key?" on the top sales banner and re-enter your purchase email or license key to instantly unlock Pro features. You can also append ?licensed=true to your URL.

Etsy 2026 Fee Breakdown

Etsy transaction fees are 6.5% charged on the total order value (item price + shipping charged). Etsy Payments processing is 3% + $0.25 in the US. The listing auto-renew fee is $0.20 per transaction. Offsite Ads fee is 15% (for shops under $10,000 in sales) or 12% (for shops over $10,000).
